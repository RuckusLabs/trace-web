Read the Privacy Policy

Before you start.

Is Trace free, and which Macs does it support?

Trace is free on the Mac App Store and requires macOS 26 or later.

What does the Mac app do beyond this demo?

Trace for Mac floats above other apps, captures beneath its window, and supports tabs and click-through locking.

Can I use Trace with Figma?

Yes. Copy your design as an image and paste it into Trace. There is no direct Figma integration.

Do screenshots leave my Mac?

No. Trace keeps screenshots on your Mac. Images in this browser demo are cleared on reload.

Can I click through the overlay?

Yes. Press Control–Command–L to lock the overlay. Activate Trace again to unlock it.

Can I compare more than one reference?

Yes. Keep references in tabs, browse thumbnails, or open another window.

How does opacity work?

Image translucence toggles between 50% and opaque. Window opacity adjusts the whole window. Matching areas turn gray with 50% translucence and 100% window opacity.

Are my screenshots saved between sessions?

Screenshots and undo history last for the session. Copy images out to keep them. Preferences and window positions persist.

Why does Trace need Screen Recording permission?

macOS requires it to capture beneath Trace. You can also paste an image from the clipboard.

Does Trace detect differences automatically?

No. Trace helps you inspect differences visually; it does not generate automated reports.
